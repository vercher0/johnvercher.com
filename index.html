Why I am running

Local government should be practical, responsive, and careful with the trust residents place in it.

John is running because the work of a city council is close to everyday life: streets, services, planning, parks, public safety, and the long-term choices that shape Mason's future.

His approach is straightforward. Listen first. Ask practical questions. Respect public dollars. Support responsible planning. Keep the work grounded in the needs of residents.

Priorities

Focused on the basics Mason residents feel every day.

Responsible Growth

Support thoughtful development that respects Mason's character and plans honestly for long-term costs.

Fiscal Stewardship

Take public dollars seriously and look for long-term value in city decisions.

Affordability

Work to lower the local tax burden where possible and help keep Mason affordable for people who want to call it home.

Infrastructure & City Services

Keep attention on roads, utilities, public works, parks, and the services residents depend on.

Transparent, Accessible Government

Make decisions easier to follow and invite residents into the process with respect.

Meet John

A Mason resident, husband, father, veteran, nurse, and public servant.

John brings a service-first mindset and a practical eye for systems: how decisions are made, how services are delivered, and how local government can earn trust through steady follow-through.
